For 8 portions, Bisto makes the gravy much quicker.

Ingredients

Sausages

16 bangers (2 per person)

Mash

2.5kg potatoes

125g butter

300ml milk

Salt and pepper

Peas

700-800g frozen peas

Small knob of butter

Onion Gravy

3-4 large onions, sliced

1 tbsp oil

5-6 heaped tbsp Bisto gravy powder

750ml-1 litre hot water

1 tbsp soy sauce (optional)

Black pepper

Method

Cook the sausages until nicely browned and cooked through.
Fry the onions slowly until golden and soft.
Mix the Bisto with a little cold water to form a smooth paste.
Add the hot water to the onions and stir in the Bisto mixture.
Add soy sauce and black pepper.
Simmer for 5-10 minutes until thickened.
Make your mash and cook the peas just before serving.

For an R85 meal, I'd plate:

2 sausages

About 250g mash

80-100g peas

Plenty of onion gravy over the sausages and mash

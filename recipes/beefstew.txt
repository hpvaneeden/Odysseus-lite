Beef Stew

3 kg chuck steak or stewing beef, cubed

4 large onions, sliced

4 cloves garlic, minced

1 litre beef stock

4 tbsp Bisto gravy powder

3 tbsp soy sauce

2 tbsp tomato paste

2 tsp black pepper

2 tsp salt (adjust to taste)

3 tbsp oil for browning

Rice

800 g uncooked long grain rice (about 4 cups)

1.6 litres water

2 tsp salt

Roast Potatoes

3 kg potatoes, cut into cubes

4 tbsp oil

2 tsp salt

1 tsp black pepper

2 tsp mixed herbs

Green Beans

1.2 kg green beans, topped and chopped

2 tbsp butter

Salt and pepper to taste

Method

Brown the beef in batches.

Remove beef and soften onions.

Add garlic and tomato paste; cook for 1 minute.

Return beef, add stock, soy sauce, pepper, and salt.

Simmer for 1½-2 hours until tender.

Mix Bisto with a little cold water and stir in during the last 15 minutes to thicken.

For Service

A good plate ratio per person would be:

250 g cooked beef stew with gravy

180-200 g cooked rice

200-250 g roast potatoes

100 g green beans

This should produce a hearty meal suitable for your R85 daily special, with enough gravy to spoon over the rice.

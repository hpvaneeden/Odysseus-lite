For a ham, onion and tomato mac & cheese for 6, I'd make it like this:

Ingredients
500g macaroni
50g butter
50g flour
750ml milk
250g cheddar cheese, grated
250–400g diced ham
1 large onion, finely diced
2 medium tomatoes, diced
1 tsp salt
½ tsp black pepper
½ tsp paprika (optional)
Method
Cook the macaroni and drain.
In a large pot, sauté the onion in a little oil until soft and lightly golden.
Add the ham and cook for 2–3 minutes.
Add the tomatoes and cook for about 5 minutes until they soften and release some of their juices.
Push everything to one side of the pot. Melt the butter, stir in the flour, and cook for 1 minute.
Gradually add the milk, stirring constantly until the sauce thickens.
Stir in the cheese until melted.
Season with salt, pepper, and paprika.
Add the cooked macaroni and mix well.
Tip

Because the tomatoes add moisture and acidity, you can increase the cheese to 300g if you want a richer, creamier result. If you're feeding hungry adults and want a bit more volume, add an extra tomato and increase the milk to 850ml.

This version is almost like a cross between a creamy mac & cheese and a mild ham pasta bake, with the onion and tomato adding sweetness and freshness.

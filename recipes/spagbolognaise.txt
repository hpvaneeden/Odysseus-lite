For 2 people, a simple spaghetti bolognese recipe:

Ingredients

200g beef mince

150g spaghetti

1 small onion, finely chopped

1 small carrot, grated (optional, but adds sweetness)

1 tbsp oil

1 clove garlic, minced (omit if needed)

1 can chopped tomatoes (400g) or 1 cup tomato puree

1 tbsp tomato paste

1 tsp mixed herbs or Italian herbs

½ tsp salt

¼ tsp black pepper

½ tsp sugar (optional)

Method

Cook the spaghetti in salted boiling water until tender. Drain and set aside.

Heat the oil in a pan and fry the onion (and carrot if using) for 3-4 minutes.

Add the mince and cook until browned, breaking it up with a spoon.

Add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds.

Stir in the tomatoes, tomato paste, herbs, salt, pepper, and sugar.

Simmer gently for 15-20 minutes until the sauce thickens.

Serve the bolognese over the spaghetti.

Hot Mess Kitchen Tip

If you have chutney, add 1 teaspoon to the sauce. It gives a nice rounded flavour and works surprisingly well with mince dishes. Also, a small splash of soy sauce adds extra depth without needing expensive ingredients.

This should give you 2 generous portions.
